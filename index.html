About Me

AR
  • Based inLisbon, Portugal
  • FocusFrontend & Design Systems
  • CurrentlyOpen to new projects

I build human-centered software at the intersection of design and engineering.

For over six years I've partnered with startups and studios to turn rough ideas into polished products — from design-system foundations and component libraries to motion-rich marketing sites and complex data interfaces.

My approach blends rigorous engineering with an eye for craft: I sweat the loading states, the empty states, and the moment a transition makes a product feel considered. When I'm not shipping, you'll find me sketching type, trail-running the Sintra hills, or contributing to open-source UI libraries.
